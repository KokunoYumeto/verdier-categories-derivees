\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-a-ii}
\noindent\hspace{1.2em}ii) Every morphism $B\longrightarrow X$, with
$B\in\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal B)$ and $X\in\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal A)$,
fits into a commutative diagram:

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-a-ii-diagram}
\begin{center}
  \begin{tikzcd}[column sep=4.2em,row sep=2.6em]
    B \arrow[r] \arrow[dr] & X &
    {B'\in\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal A\cap\mathcal B)} \\
    & B' \arrow[u] &
  \end{tikzcd}
  \quad .
\end{center}

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-a-definition}
The full triangulated subcategories $\mathcal A$ of $\mathcal D$ having the
two equivalent properties above are called \emph{right
$\mathcal B$-localizing subcategories}.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-b}
\par\noindent b) If $\mathcal A$ is right $\mathcal B$-localizing, the
canonical functor

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-b-functor}
\[
  \mathcal A/(\mathcal A\cap\mathcal B)
  \longrightarrow\mathcal D/\mathcal B
\]
is fully faithful and injective on objects.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-c}
\par\noindent c) The following conditions are equivalent:

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-c-i}
\par\noindent\hspace{1.2em}i) $\mathcal A$ is right
$\mathcal B$-localizing.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-c-ii}
\par\noindent\hspace{1.2em}ii) Let
$Q\colon\mathcal D/(\mathcal A\cap\mathcal B)\longrightarrow
\mathcal D/\mathcal B$ be the canonical functor. The subcategory
$\mathcal A/(\mathcal A\cap\mathcal B)\longrightarrow
\mathcal D/(\mathcal A\cap\mathcal B)$ consists of objects that are
$Q$-free on the right.
\endgroup

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-proof-a}
The equivalence in a) follows immediately from TRIII and the definitions
of the systems $S_{\mathcal D}(\mathcal B)$ and
$S_{\mathcal A}(\mathcal A\cap\mathcal B)$ (2.1.7).

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-proof-b-setup}
We prove b). Let $X$ and $Y$ be two objects of $\mathcal A$ (and hence of
$\mathcal A/(\mathcal A\cap\mathcal B)$).

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-proof-b-injectivity}
First we show that the homomorphism
\[
  \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal A/(\mathcal A\cap\mathcal B)}(X,Y)
  \longrightarrow
  \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D/\mathcal B}(X,Y)
\]
is injective.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-proof-b-quotient-functors}
Denote by
$Q_1\colon\mathcal A\longrightarrow
\mathcal A/(\mathcal A\cap\mathcal B)$ and
$Q_2\colon\mathcal D\longrightarrow\mathcal D/\mathcal B$ the canonical
quotient functors.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-proof-b-fraction}
An element
$m\in\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal A/(\mathcal A\cap\mathcal B)}(X,Y)$ can
always be written in the form $m=Q_1(s)^{-1}Q_1(f)$,
$s\in S_{\mathcal A}(\mathcal A\cap\mathcal B)$ (2.2.2). Its image in
$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D/\mathcal B}(X,Y)$ is then
$Q_2(s)^{-1}Q_2(f)$.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-proof-b-zero-criterion}
By (2.2.4, a)), this image is zero if and only if there exists a
commutative diagram:

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-proof-b-zero-diagram}
\begin{center}
  \begin{tikzcd}[column sep=3.7em,row sep=2.5em]
    & {} \arrow[r,"u"] & {} \\
    X \arrow[ur,"f"] && Y \arrow[ul,"s"] \arrow[u,"t"'] &
    {t\in S_{\mathcal D}(\mathcal B)}
  \end{tikzcd}
\end{center}
\endgroup
