\medskip\noindent\textbf{4.3.3.}\quad
We are particularly interested in the terms
$E\!\left[\begin{smallmatrix}n\\p-r+1,p,p+1,p+r\end{smallmatrix}\right]$,
$p\in\mathbb Z$, $r\in\mathbb Z$, $r>0$, since, under suitable
hypotheses, these will allow us to determine the associated graded object of
$H^n(-\infty,+\infty)$. To return to the notation of \emph{loc. cit.}, we
set:

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4-4-3-3-1}
\[
\left\{
\begin{aligned}
E\!\left[\begin{smallmatrix}n\\p-r,p-1,p,p+r-1\end{smallmatrix}\right]
  &=\mathrm I_r^{-p,n+p},\\
&\textit{i.e.}:\\[-2pt]
\mathrm I_r^{p,q}
  &=E\!\left[\begin{smallmatrix}p+q\\-p-r,-p-1,-p,-p+r-1\end{smallmatrix}\right],
  &&p,q\in\mathbb Z,\quad 1\leq r\leq+\infty,\\
&\text{The corresponding differentials will be denoted by:}\\[-2pt]
d_r\colon\mathrm I_r^{p,q}&\longrightarrow
  \mathrm I_r^{p+r,q-r+1}.
\end{aligned}
\right.
\tag{4.3.3.1}
\]

We shall very often use the notation:

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4-4-3-3-2}
\[
\left\{
\begin{aligned}
E\!\left[\begin{smallmatrix}n\\p-r+1,p,p+1,p+r\end{smallmatrix}\right]
  &=\mathrm{II}_{r+1}^{n-p,p},\\
&\textit{i.e.}:\\[-2pt]
\mathrm{II}_r^{p,q}
  &=E\!\left[\begin{smallmatrix}p+q\\q-r+2,q,q+1,q+r-1\end{smallmatrix}\right],
  &&p,q\in\mathbb Z,\quad 2\leq r\leq+\infty,\\
&\text{The corresponding differentials will be denoted by:}\\[-2pt]
d_r\colon\mathrm{II}_r^{p,q}&\longrightarrow
  \mathrm{II}_r^{p+r,q-r+1}.
\end{aligned}
\right.
\tag{4.3.3.2}
\]

We shall specify in each case which notation is being used. Notice that when
notation (4.3.3.1) is used, the terms $\mathrm I_r^{p,q}$ are defined for
$r\geq1$, whereas when notation (4.3.3.2) is used, the terms
$\mathrm{II}_r^{p,q}$ are defined only for $r\geq2$.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4-4-3-3-spectral-sequence}
The collection of terms
$E\!\left[\begin{smallmatrix}n\\p-r+1,p,p+1,p+r\end{smallmatrix}\right]$,
$n,p\in\mathbb Z$, $1\leq r\leq+\infty$, equipped with the corresponding
differentials, is called the \emph{spectral sequence associated with the
spectral object} $(H^n(p,q),\delta(p,q,r))$.
