\noindent\textbf{3.4.4.}\quad
When countable direct sums are representable in $C$, there is a proposition
analogous to Proposition (3.4.3) for the functor $K_{\Sigma}F$. The proof is
obtained by replacing products by sums in the proof of Proposition (3.4.3).
