\medskip\noindent\textbf{4.4.4.}\quad
Let $(H^n(p,q),\delta)$ and $(H'{}^n(p,q),\delta')$ be two spectral objects
with values in $\mathcal A$, and let
$m^n(p,q)\colon H^n(p,q)\longrightarrow H'{}^n(p,q)$ be a morphism of
spectral objects. Such a morphism induces a morphism of the corresponding
spectral families, i.e. a family of morphisms:
\[
m\!\left[\begin{smallmatrix}n\\p,q,r,s\end{smallmatrix}\right]
\colon
E\!\left[\begin{smallmatrix}n\\p,q,r,s\end{smallmatrix}\right]
\longrightarrow
E'\!\left[\begin{smallmatrix}n\\p,q,r,s\end{smallmatrix}\right]
\]
which commute with the differentials. In particular, the morphism $m(p,q)$
induces a morphism of the corresponding spectral sequences:
\[
m_r^{p,q}\colon E_r^{p,q}\longrightarrow E'{}_{r}^{p,q}.
\]
(Here we put $E_r^{p,q}=\mathrm I_r^{p,q}$ or
$E_r^{p,q}=\mathrm{II}_r^{p,q}$, according as one or the other notation of
(4.3.3) is used.)

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4-4-4-5}
\medskip\noindent\textbf{Proposition 4.4.5.}\quad
\textit{Suppose that the spectral objects}
\[
(H^n(p,q),\delta)
\qquad\textit{and}\qquad
(H'{}^n(p,q),\delta')
\]
\textit{are stationary and that, for a suitable integer $r$, the morphism}
\[
m_r^{p,q}\colon E_r^{p,q}\longrightarrow E'{}_{r}^{p,q}
\]
\textit{is an isomorphism for every pair $p,q$. Then, for every $r'\geq r$,
the morphism}
\[
m_{r'}^{p,q}\colon E_{r'}^{p,q}\longrightarrow E'{}_{r'}^{p,q}
\]
\textit{is an isomorphism. The morphism}
\[
m^n(-\infty,+\infty)\colon H^n(-\infty,+\infty)
\longrightarrow H'{}^n(-\infty,+\infty)
\]
\textit{is an isomorphism of filtered objects.}

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4-4-4-5-proof}
Indeed, the $m_r^{p,q}$ define an isomorphism between the graded differential
objects $(E_r^{p,q},d_r)$ and $(E'{}_{r}^{p,q},d'_r)$. They therefore induce
an isomorphism on the cohomology objects $E_{r+1}^{p,q}$ and
$E'{}_{r+1}^{p,q}$, whence the first assertion by induction on $r'$. Since
the spectral objects are stationary, the morphism
\[
m_\infty^{p,q}\colon E_\infty^{p,q}\longrightarrow
E'{}_{\infty}^{p,q}
\]
is an isomorphism. The $E_\infty^{p,q}$ (respectively,
$E'{}_{\infty}^{p,q}$) are the associated graded objects of the filtered
objects $H^n(-\infty,+\infty)$ (respectively,
$H'{}^n(-\infty,+\infty)$). Since the filtrations of these objects are
stationary, it follows that
\[
m^n(-\infty,+\infty)\colon H^n(-\infty,+\infty)
\longrightarrow H'{}^n(-\infty,+\infty)
\]
is an isomorphism of filtered objects.
