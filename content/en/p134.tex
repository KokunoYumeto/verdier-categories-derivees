\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-15}
\noindent\textbf{2.1.15.}\quad
\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-15-associated-system}%
Let $\mathcal D'$ be a full triangulated subcategory of a triangulated
category $\mathcal D$. The multiplicative system $S(\mathcal D')$ (2.1.7) is
called the \emph{multiplicative system associated with the subcategory
$\mathcal D'$}.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-16}
\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Proposition 2.1.16.}\quad
\begingroup\itshape
\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-16-setup}%
Let $F\colon\mathcal D_1\to\mathcal D_2$ be an exact functor between two
triangulated categories (1.1.3). Let $S(F)$ be the set of morphisms of
$\mathcal D_1$ that are carried by $F$ to isomorphisms of $\mathcal D_2$.
\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-16-kernel-subcategory}%
Let $\mathcal D'(F)$ be the full subcategory of $\mathcal D_1$ defined by the
objects of $\mathcal D_1$ that are carried by $F$ to zero objects of
$\mathcal D_2$.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-16-a}
\par\noindent\hspace*{2em}a)\enspace The subcategory $\mathcal D'(F)$ is a
strictly full triangulated subcategory (every object of $\mathcal D_1$
isomorphic to an object of $\mathcal D'(F)$ is an object of
$\mathcal D'(F)$) and is saturated.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-16-b}
\par\noindent\hspace*{2em}b)\enspace The set $S(F)$ is a saturated
multiplicative system of $\mathcal D_1$.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-16-c}
\par\noindent\hspace*{2em}c)\enspace The set $S(F)$ is the multiplicative
system associated with the subcategory $\mathcal D'(F)$ (2.1.15).
\par\endgroup

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-16-proof-omitted}
\noindent The proof is easy and is left to the reader, who may prove the
following proposition in the same way:

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-17}
\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Proposition 2.1.17.}\quad
\begingroup\itshape
\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-17-setup}%
Let $H\colon\mathcal D\to\mathcal A$ be a cohomological functor from a
triangulated category to an abelian category. Let $S(H)$ be the set of
morphisms of $\mathcal D$ that, together with their translates, are carried
to isomorphisms of $\mathcal A$.
\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-17-kernel-subcategory}%
Let $\mathcal D'(H)$ be the full subcategory of $\mathcal D$ defined by the
objects of $\mathcal D$ that, together with their translates, are carried to
zero objects of $\mathcal A$.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-17-a}
\par\noindent\hspace*{2em}a)\enspace The subcategory $\mathcal D'(H)$ is a
strictly full triangulated subcategory and is saturated.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-17-b}
\par\noindent\hspace*{2em}b)\enspace The set $S(H)$ is a saturated
multiplicative system of $\mathcal D$.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-1-17-c}
\par\noindent\hspace*{2em}c)\enspace The set $S(H)$ is the multiplicative
system associated with the subcategory $\mathcal D'(H)$ (2.1.15).
\par\endgroup

\noindent\textbf{2.2.\quad Construction of the localized category.}

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-1}
\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{2.2.1.}\quad
\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-1-slice-categories}%
Let $\mathcal C$ be a category (not necessarily additive) and $S$ a
multiplicative system of $\mathcal C$. For every object $X$ of $\mathcal C$,
denote by $S/X$ (resp. $X\backslash S$) the category whose objects are the
morphisms in $S$ with target $X$ (resp. with source $X$)
\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-1-morphism-diagrams}%
and whose morphisms are the commutative diagrams:
