\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch3d-1-2-3-a-cont}
\noindent\textit{are strictly fully faithful. They are injective on objects and
thus realize the categories $K^{,*}(\mathcal A)/\operatorname{Ac}(\mathcal A)$
as strictly full triangulated subcategories of $D(\mathcal A)$. An object
$X$ of $D(\mathcal A)$ is an object of
$K^{,b}(\mathcal A)/\operatorname{Ac}(\mathcal A)$ (respectively,
$K^{,+}(\mathcal A)/\operatorname{Ac}(\mathcal A)$, respectively,
$K^{,-}(\mathcal A)/\operatorname{Ac}(\mathcal A)$) if and only if
$H^i(X)=0$ for $|i|$ large (respectively, $i$ small, respectively, $i$
large).}
