the complex $Y$.

Denote by

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4d-4-1-2-4}
\begin{equation}
\varphi\bigl((p,q),(p',q')\bigr)\colon
Y(p,q)\longrightarrow Y(p',q'),\qquad p\leq p',\quad q\leq q',
\tag{4.1.2.4}
\end{equation}
the homotopy class of the morphism of complexes

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4d-4-1-2-5}
\begin{equation}
\psi\bigl((p,q),(p',q')\bigr)\colon
Y(p,q)\longrightarrow Y(p',q'),
\tag{4.1.2.5}
\end{equation}
whose components are identity morphisms whenever the source and target are
nonzero objects. It is immediate that this defines a functor from
$\mathcal{F}\ell_1(\widetilde{\mathbb Z})$ to $K(\mathcal A)$.

Let $(p,q,r)$ be an element of
$\mathcal{F}\ell_2(\widetilde{\mathbb Z})$. Denote by

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4d-4-1-2-6}
\begin{equation}
\delta(p,q,r)\colon Y(q,r)\longrightarrow Y(p,q)[1]
\tag{4.1.2.6}
