\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-11}
\noindent\textbf{Corollary 2.2.11.}\quad
\begingroup\itshape
\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-11-setup}%
Let $\mathcal D$ be a triangulated category and
$\mathcal B\subset\mathcal D$ a full triangulated subcategory.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-11-a}
\par\noindent a) The kernel of the quotient functor
$Q\colon\mathcal D\longrightarrow\mathcal D/\mathcal B$ is the smallest
strictly full saturated triangulated subcategory containing $\mathcal B$.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-11-b-order-isomorphism}
\par\noindent b) The map
$\mathcal B\longmapsto S(\mathcal B)$, which assigns to every strictly
full saturated triangulated subcategory its saturated multiplicative
system (2.1.8), is an isomorphism of ordered sets from the set of strictly
full saturated triangulated subcategories, ordered by inclusion, onto the
set of saturated multiplicative systems of $\mathcal D$, ordered by
inclusion.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-11-b-inverse}
The inverse map assigns to every saturated multiplicative system the kernel
of the corresponding localization functor.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-11-c-factorization}
\par\noindent c) An exact (resp. cohomological) functor
$F\colon\mathcal D\longrightarrow\mathcal D'$ whose kernel contains the
subcategory $\mathcal B$ factors uniquely through
$Q\colon\mathcal D\longrightarrow\mathcal D/\mathcal B$ as a functor
$G\colon\mathcal D/\mathcal B\longrightarrow\mathcal D'$ which is exact
(resp. cohomological).

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-11-c-morphism-lifting}
A morphism of exact (resp. cohomological) functors whose kernels contain
the subcategory $\mathcal B$ arises uniquely from a morphism between the
factorizations.
\endgroup

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-11-easy-consequences}
These are easy consequences of theorem (2.2.6).

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-12}
\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{2.2.12. Comments on property TRIV.}
\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-12-pretriangulated-category}%
Let us call a strict $\mathbb Z$-category equipped with a family of
triangles satisfying properties TRI, TRII, and TRIII a
\emph{pre-triangulated category}.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-12-induced-notions}
We define the notions of \emph{exact functors},
\emph{cohomological functors}, and \emph{multiplicative systems} in the
same way as for triangulated categories.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-12-localization}
Let $S$ be a multiplicative system (2.1.4) in a pre-triangulated category
$\mathcal D$. The localized category $\mathcal D(S^{-1})$ admits one and
only one pre-triangulated category structure such that the localization
functor is exact.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-12-kernel-subcategory}
The full subcategory on the objects of $\mathcal D$ annihilated by the
localization functor is a full pre-triangulated subcategory.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-12-triv-necessity}
In practice one needs to know how, conversely, to associate a suitable
saturated multiplicative system with a pre-triangulated subcategory. The
editor knows how to do this only by using property TRIV.

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-2-12-triv-role}
This property, rather technical in nature, has therefore so far served
mainly to obtain corollary (2.2.11) from theorem (2.2.6).
