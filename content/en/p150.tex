\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch2-2-3-5-left-localizing-definition}
\medskip
A full triangulated subcategory $\mathcal A$ of $\mathcal D$ is said to be
\emph{$\mathcal B$-localizing on the left} if the subcategory
$\mathcal A^\circ$ of $\mathcal D^\circ$ is
$\mathcal B^\circ$-localizing on the right.
