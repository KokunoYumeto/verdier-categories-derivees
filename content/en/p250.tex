\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4d-4-6-9-derived-functors}
Since the double complex $I^{\bullet\bullet}$ is a Cartan--Eilenberg
injective resolution of $Y$, the complex $H_1^r(I^{\bullet\bullet})$ is an
injective resolution of the object $H^r(Y)$; consequently, there is an
isomorphism

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4d-4-6-9-4}
\[
H_{\mathcal B}^{p}\left(
F^{q-r,\bullet}(H_1^r(I^{\bullet\bullet}))
\right)
\xrightarrow{\sim}
R^pF^{q-r}(H^r(Y)),
\tag{4.6.9.4}
\]
where $R^pF^{q-r}\colon\mathcal A\longrightarrow\mathcal B$ denotes the
$p$th right derived functor (in the sense of [1]) of the functor
$F^{q-r}$ restricted to the category $\mathcal A$. Thus, when the
morphism (4.6.9.3) is an isomorphism, there is an isomorphism

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4d-4-6-9-5}
\[
(\mathrm{C.E.})_2^{p,q}\xrightarrow{\sim}
\prod_{r\in\mathbb Z}R^pF^{q-r}(H^r(Y)).
\tag{4.6.9.5}
\]

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4d-4-6-10}
\medskip\noindent\textbf{4.6.10.}\quad
Consider the case in which the category $\mathcal B$ is the category
$\mathbf{Ab}$ of abelian groups belonging to a suitable universe and in
which the functor

\[
F\colon D(\mathcal A)\longrightarrow\mathbf{Ab}
\]
is the functor

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4d-4-6-10-ext0}
\[
Y\longmapsto F(Y)=\operatorname{Ext}^{0}(Z,Y),
\]
where $Z$ is an object of $D(\mathcal A)$. Let $Y$ be a complex of
$\mathcal A$, and let
$\varepsilon\colon Y\longrightarrow I^{\bullet\bullet}$ be a
Cartan--Eilenberg injective resolution. The morphism (4.6.9.3) is then an
isomorphism (4.6.3), and consequently there is an isomorphism

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4d-4-6-10-1}
\[
(\mathrm{C.E.})_2^{p,q}\xrightarrow{\sim}
\prod_{r\in\mathbb Z}
\operatorname{Ext}^{p}\bigl(H^{r-q}(Z),H^r(Y)\bigr).
\tag{4.6.10.1}
\]

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4d-4-6-10-stationarity}
Moreover, the Cartan--Eilenberg spectral sequence relative to $F$ is
stationary when $Y$ is an object of
$\operatorname{comp}^{+}(\mathcal A)$ and $Z$ is an object of
$D^{-}(\mathcal A)$, or when the Cartan--Eilenberg injective resolution
has finite length. In addition, under these hypotheses the abutment of
this spectral sequence is, by (4.6.8),

\VerdierSemanticAnchor{ch4d-4-6-10-abutment}
\[
\prod_{r\in\mathbb Z}
\operatorname{Ext}^{p}\bigl(H^{r-q}(Z),H^r(Y)\bigr)
\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{Ext}^{p+q}(Z,Y).
\]
